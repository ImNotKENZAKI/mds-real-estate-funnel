MDS CINEMATIC VIDEO STORY — 2026-09-15

Placement
---------
Between the Property-to-Pipeline system section and Solutions.

Behavior
--------
- Sticky full-screen video story.
- YouTube video 64HUMVre1QM lazy-loads as a muted looping background.
- Four editorial story beats change with scroll.
- Watch with Sound opens an immersive 16:9 presentation mode, restarts the film, unmutes it, and hides story text.
- Custom play/pause, sound and scrub controls.
- Return to Story restores muted background mode at the same page position.
- Escape closes immersive playback.
- Reduced-motion users get a static final message instead of a long scroll sequence.

Design constraints
------------------
- No chapter pills, generic cards, Lucide icons, or template-style glass UI.
- Uses the MDS signal-line language, navy/gold/blue palette and editorial typography.
- Existing hero, system animation, forms, Step 2 and calendar logic were not redesigned.
