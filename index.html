FAQ

What to expect before you request an audit.

The audit is designed to understand your current setup before any rebuild or tool recommendation.

Do I need to replace my current CRM?+

Not necessarily. We first review whether your current CRM can support the lead journey you need before recommending a change.

Can you work with our existing tools?+

Yes. The goal is to improve what already makes sense and only introduce new tools when there is a clear reason.

Do you also build the landing pages?+

Yes. The landing experience can be custom-coded so the campaign message, form, follow-up, and routing work as one system.

How long does implementation take?+

Timing depends on scope and existing systems. After the audit we can recommend a realistic implementation plan.

What happens after the audit?+

We identify the gaps, clarify priorities, and outline practical next steps. You decide whether you want MDS to implement them.
